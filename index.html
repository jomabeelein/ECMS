Campus Safety & Culture

ECMS Cardinal School Expectations & Safety Policies

Review these core Tulsa Public Schools & ECMS campus safety policies together with your student!

1. NO FIGHT ZONE Policy

ECMS is a NO FIGHT ZONE. Zero tolerance for fighting on any Tulsa Public Schools property.

2. Student ID Badge Visible

Student ID cards must be worn and visible around the neck at all times while on campus for student safety.

3. NO Hoodies Allowed

No hoodies allowed at all on campus (sweatshirts with hoods are strictly prohibited inside the school building).

4. Backpack Policy

Only clear backpacks or small personal bags under 14 inches are permitted on campus.

5. Food Inside Cafeteria Only

All food, snacks, and drinks must be consumed inside the cafeteria only. No food in hallways or classrooms.

6. Electronics Put Away

No cell phones, headphones, earbuds, or other personal electronics during school hours. Keep devices stored away.

7. TPS Dress Code Adherence

Full compliance with official Tulsa Public Schools dress code guidelines every school day.
